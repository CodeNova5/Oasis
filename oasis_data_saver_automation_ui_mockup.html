9. Safety and Rollback

            FAIL-SAFE CONTROLS
          

          

            Immediate recovery actions and honest attribution warnings for shared CDNs, HTTP/2/QUIC multiplexing, unknown process owners, or weak SNI.
          

        


        

          
          
          
        

      


      
      

        

          
Shared CDN Endpoints

          
Multiple apps can share edge CDNs; domain-only blocks may impact unrelated traffic without path/group scope.

        

        

          
Multiplexed Streams

          
HTTP/2 and QUIC connections multiplex streams; per-connection rate caps affect all streams on that socket.

        

        

          
Unknown Process Owner

          
Flows lacking confident PID/service attribution are labeled Unknown rather than forced to zero.

        

        

          
Weak or Hidden SNI

          
Encrypted ClientHello (ECH), DoH, or direct IPs limit domain certainty; confidence stays Possible or Unknown.
